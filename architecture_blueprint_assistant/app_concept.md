# Application Concept – StudyConnect

## Application
StudyConnect is a collaborative learning platform designed to help students connect with peers, form study groups, share academic resources, and schedule study sessions. The platform aims to improve learning efficiency by enabling students to collaborate easily, access shared materials, and receive academic support through peer interaction and tutoring.

## Core Features
- **Study Group Creation** – Students can create and join study groups for specific courses or subjects.
- **Resource Sharing** – Upload and download study materials such as notes, summaries, and past exams.
- **Study Session Scheduling** – Built-in calendar to organize and manage group study sessions.
- **Real-Time Chat** – Messaging system for communication within study groups.
- **Search and Matching** – Find study partners based on course, schedule, or interests.
- **Notifications System** – Alerts for new resources, group updates, and upcoming study sessions.

## Users
- **Students**: Use the platform to find study partners, collaborate, and share learning resources.
- **Tutors**: Offer tutoring sessions and share academic guidance or study materials.
- **Administrators**: Manage the platform, monitor content, and ensure proper system operation.

## Constraints
- **Scalability**: The system should support at least 50,000 concurrent users during peak academic periods.
- **Platform Support**: Web-based platform with mobile-responsive design for smartphones and tablets.
- **Data Privacy**: Compliance with privacy regulations and secure storage of user data.
- **Performance**: Fast search and matching functionality with minimal latency.
